================================================================================
          EDU_TECH: TEACHER-STUDENT INSTITUTIONAL CONNECTION GUIDE
================================================================================

This guide explains how the newly implemented Teacher-Student connection works 
within the Edu_Tech platform. Linking is handled automatically based on the 
"College Name" provided in profiles.

---
STEP 1: ROLE-BASED LOGIN
---
- Navigate to the login page.
- Use the new "Role Selector" to choose either 'Student' or 'Teacher'.
- Log in with your credentials.

---
STEP 2: PROFILE SETUP (THE LINKING THREAD)
---
The connection is established using the "College Name" field:
- TEACHERS: Must complete their profile at /teacher_profile and specify their 
  College/School.
- STUDENTS: Must complete their profile at /profile and specify the SAME 
  College/School name.

---
STEP 3: TEACHER ACTIONS (POSTING WORK)
---
On the Teacher Dashboard (/teacher_home):
1. Use the "Tasks" section to assign work (e.g., "Math Assignment 1").
2. Use the "Announcements" section to post news (e.g., "Holiday on Monday").
3. When saved, these are automatically broadcasted ONLY to students of the 
   same college.

---
STEP 4: STUDENT VIEW (THE ECOSYSTEM)
---
On the Student Dashboard (/dashboard), a new section "My Campus Connection" 
automatically displays:
1. OUR TEACHERS: A directory of all teachers registered from the student's college.
2. ASSIGNED WORK: A list of tasks posted by those teachers.
3. ANNOUNCEMENTS: Real-time updates from their college faculty.

---
TECHNICAL OVERVIEW
---
- Database Model: 'TeacherWork' schema now tracks 'collegeName' and 'teacherId'.
- Security: Redirections and data fetching are scoped by session roles and 
  institutional identifiers to ensure privacy between different schools.
================================================================================
